### Item 1 — IETF WIMSE evidence-layer cluster: EMILIA Protocol (Schrock) + PEDIGREE/root comparative mapping (Rampalli) — new, strongest signal this run

**PART A**
1. **Title:** Action Evidence Graphs and Evidence Policy Replay for High-Risk Agent Actions (EP-AEG, draft-schrock-ep-action-evidence-graph-00); A Layered Requirements Mapping for Cross-Organization Agent Delegation (draft-rampalli-cross-org-delegation-mapping-03)
2. **Source:** IETF WIMSE working-group mailing list / Internet-Drafts (primary)
3. **Publication date:** EP-AEG published 2026-07 (~1 week old at run time); Rampalli mapping -01 published 2026-07-06, revised to -03 by 2026-07-13; human-authorization row contributed on-list 2026-07-05
4. **Link:** https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-schrock-ep-action-evidence-graph/ ; https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-rampalli-cross-org-delegation-mapping/03/
5. **Jurisdiction / institution:** IETF (WIMSE WG mailing list; both documents are individual Internet-Drafts, no formal IETF standing)
6. **Factual summary:** EP-AEG defines a content-addressed evidence graph over an AI agent's action plus a deterministic offline "Evidence Policy Replay" yielding one of five closed verdicts including "admissible"; the Rampalli mapping separately evaluates a per-hop delegation chain ("PEDIGREE") against a distinct "named-human authorization root" layer (EMILIA Protocol binding + evidence-graph drafts), explicitly modeling a named, accountable human or M-of-N quorum of distinct humans as authorizing "this exact operation before execution... asserted as human authority, not organizational policy."
7. **Why selected:** `[PROXIMITY SIGNAL — RJB PUBLICATION: P1, P2, P3]` — this is the sharpest external convergence on ABCF's core architecture found to date. See Part B.
8. **ABCF component(s):** rooted human authorization; named accountable human integrators; admissibility checks; execution gates; provenance chains; structural causal identifiability (partial)
9. **Relevance level:** Direct relevance

**PART B**
- **Coherence:** Very high. The mapping's own framing — two independent "evidence layers" with "independent failure behavior, joined by action digest" — is structurally the same move ABCF makes in separating delegation topology from admissibility/identifiability.
- **Alignment:** The strongest single alignment signal in the corpus to date. The named-human-authorization row states the human root "asserted as human authority, not organizational policy" — this is a near-verbatim external statement of ABCF's rooted-human-authorization / named-integrator-topology distinction (authority must trace to an identifiable human, not merely to organizational process). EP-AEG's five-verdict closed set (including "admissible") deterministically evaluated offline against a relying-party-supplied policy is structurally close to AWAP (AI-Assisted Work Admissibility Packet) — an artifact is not usable until it clears an explicit admissibility gate, and "verified" and "accepted" are explicitly held as separate, non-implying results.
- **Infringement / tension:** The convergence is architectural, not identifiability-grounded. Neither draft establishes a causal-identifiability precondition — "admissible" in EP-AEG is a policy-replay verdict over evidence completeness (which signed artifacts are present and how they compose), not a determination that causal contribution can be traced. The gap ABCF names (structural attribution indeterminacy as a distinct, sometimes unresolvable class) is not engaged. This is the precise shape of prior runs' finding — authorization-before-execution gating maturing without the identifiability condition — but now expressed with more architectural precision (closed verdict sets, disclosure-independent graph identity, fail-closed invariants) than any prior-run item.
- **Known facts:** Draft dates, verdict-set structure, named-human-authorization framing, WIMSE mailing-list provenance, non-standing status of Internet-Drafts.
- **Inferences:** This cluster (NIST → IETF delegation-chain drafts → now IETF evidence-layer/human-root drafts) is converging fastest of any tracked node; the shift from single-hop OAuth extensions (Run 6–7) to a formal two-layer evidence architecture with a dedicated human-authorization root (Run 9) suggests the field is independently re-deriving the chain/root separation ABCF treats as foundational.
- **Unknowns:** Whether EMILIA Protocol or PEDIGREE will be adopted as a formal IETF work item or remain individual drafts; whether any future revision introduces an identifiability precondition.
- **ABCF classification:** Coherent but incomplete

The paper does not use ABCF terminology (structural causal identifiability, mixed-cycle interaction topology) but arrives at the same class of impossibility from a measurement-theoretic angle (a model that hides a property is behaviorally indistinguishable from one that lacks it).
