### Item 1 — IETF WIMSE delegation-mapping cluster: draft-rampalli-cross-org-delegation-mapping revised, list discussion continues (carried, advancing)

**PART A**
1. **Title:** A Layered Requirements Mapping for Cross-Organization Agent Delegation (draft-rampalli-cross-org-delegation-mapping-03)
2. **Source:** IETF WIMSE working-group Internet-Draft and mailing-list archive (primary)
3. **Publication date:** 2026-07-06 (revision -03; thread discussion continuing through the run window)
4. **Link:** https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-rampalli-cross-org-delegation-mapping-03.html ; https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/msg/wimse/9NfrUeYPPEgsizNNqrwx9fq_Qao/
5. **Jurisdiction / institution:** IETF (WIMSE WG mailing list; individual Internet-Draft, no formal IETF standing)
6. **Factual summary:** The draft formally maps two evidence layers for cross-organization AI agent delegation — a per-hop delegation chain (PEDIGREE) and a named-human-authorization root (the EMILIA Protocol binding/evidence-graph drafts) — against the nine requirements of draft-reece-wimse-cross-org-delegation, and records a verifier-facing composition model in which key possession, delegated authority, and pre-execution human authorization are "diagnostically separate inputs with independent failure behavior, joined by action digest."
7. **Why selected:** `[PROXIMITY SIGNAL — RJB PUBLICATION: P1, P2]` — direct continuation and formalization of the cluster flagged as the strongest convergence across all nine prior runs.
8. **ABCF component(s):** rooted human authorization; named accountable human integrators; execution gates; admissibility checks; containment boundaries
9. **Relevance level:** Direct relevance

**PART B**
- **Coherence:** High. "Diagnostically separate inputs with independent failure behavior" is structurally the same move as ABCF's CVG Separation Invariant (VALID ≢ MANDATE) — the draft is independently insisting that possession, delegation, and human authorization must not be allowed to substitute for one another in a verifier's reasoning.
- **Alignment:** The composition model treats pre-execution human authorization as a distinct, non-collapsible input rather than a property inferable from the delegation chain — this is architecturally close to ABCF's Authority Activation / Leadership Artifact concept (interventions executable only through explicit authorization act).
- **Infringement / tension:** Still no causal-identifiability precondition. The mapping evaluates whether authorization *exists* and *composes* correctly, not whether the underlying causal contribution of the AI-assisted action is structurally identifiable. This is the same gap named in Run 9 — now expressed at even finer resolution, since the draft explicitly separates authorization-existence from authorization-sufficiency without naming the latter as a distinct problem class.
- **Known facts:** Draft revision history (-01 → -03 within roughly one month), mailing-list contribution provenance, non-standing status of the Internet-Draft.
- **Inferences:** The cluster is maturing from framing (Run 8–9) into worked verifier logic (Run 10) — the "joined by action digest" language suggests implementers are now specifying the binding mechanism, not just the taxonomy.
- **Unknowns:** Whether this mapping will be adopted as a WIMSE WG item or remain an individual contribution; whether a future revision introduces an identifiability precondition.
- **ABCF classification:** Coherent but incomplete

### Item 2 — "The Khipu Problem: Institutional Legibility Under Distributed Cognition" (Tallam, arXiv) — new

**PART A**
1. **Title:** The Khipu Problem: Institutional Legibility Under Distributed Cognition
2. **Source:** arXiv (cs.CY), preprint
3. **Publication date:** 2026-06 (arXiv:2606.12414)
4. **Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.12414
5. **Jurisdiction / institution:** Academic preprint, independent author (Krti Tallam)
6. **Factual summary:** The paper argues that as AI systems distribute cognition across models, tools, retrieval layers, runtime policy, and human intervention, the central governance problem shifts from data retention to interpretive legibility — records can survive while the reading practice needed to interpret them decays — and calls for institutional environments that preserve "requester truth, boundary truth, authority continuity, evidential scope, runtime lineage, and bounded receipts for consequential action."
7. **Why selected:** `[PROXIMITY SIGNAL — RJB PUBLICATION]` — independently arrives at a structurally convergent framing of authority continuity and bounded, authority-governed receipts for consequential action.
8. **ABCF component(s):** provenance chains; decision traceability; containment boundaries; admissibility checks
9. **Relevance level:** Indirect relevance

**PART B**
- **Coherence:** Moderate-to-high. "Authority continuity" and "bounded receipts for consequential action" echo ABCF's Probe Execution Log (PEL) and Contraction Guarantee concepts (a governed expansion of authority must leave a reconstructible record and must be restored).
- **Alignment:** The paper's core move — from "was it retained" to "can it still be read as one governable object" — supports ABCF's position that observability and traceability are not the same as structural identifiability of causal contribution.
- **Infringement / tension:** The paper is diagnostic rather than architectural: it names the failure mode (interpretive decay) and lists desiderata but does not propose a formal admissibility gate or an identifiability condition comparable to SLT. It also does not distinguish epistemic uncertainty from the ontological attribution limit ABCF's Structural Indeterminacy names.
- **Known facts:** Author, venue, publication window, the six preservation requirements listed in the conclusion.
- **Inferences:** This is a second, independently-authored instance (alongside the July 2025–2026 WIMSE cluster) of researchers separately re-deriving the need for a named, bounded, authority-tied record of consequential AI action — weak but real corroboration that the field is converging on this shape of problem from multiple directions.
- **Unknowns:** Whether this is a standalone conceptual paper or the first of a series; no formal model or theorem is offered.
- **ABCF classification:** Coherent but incomplete
